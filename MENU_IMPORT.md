## Interpretation notes

- Names and prices are transcribed as printed. “Lays Chicken” stays under French Fries; “Fully Loaded Fried Chicken” stays under Pizza; “Warrier Combo” retains the printed name.
- Portion sizes are part of the product name. Quantity 1 means one listed portion or bucket, not one individual chicken piece.
- Birthday Top Load allows separate rice/noodle choices for its two portions. Veg Load includes the card’s unspecified Mojito; no flavour or extra charge has been invented.
- There are no added standalone drink products or invented quarter-grill prices. Those items appear only within the printed combos.
- Header and sidebar use the supplied warrior artwork through CSS cropping. Category artwork comes from the menu cards; categories without supplied food photos use branded artwork instead of unrelated sample food photos.

## Upgrade behaviour

- Version 1.1.0 imports this menu once on desktop startup and makes a consistent before-client-menu SQLite backup first. Existing accounts, sales snapshots, invoice sequence and printer/tax settings are preserved.
- Browser demo history is cleared once and its old localStorage value retained under chefwarriors-demo-v1-before-client-import.
- Subsequent app launches preserve edits made to the imported menu. Restoring an old pre-import backup re-applies the catalogue update.
- Existing desktop sales cannot safely be classified as test or genuine from their product names, so none are automatically deleted.
